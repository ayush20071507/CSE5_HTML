Famous Monument

About:

The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world. It is located in Agra, India, and was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Made of beautiful white marble, the Taj Mahal is admired for its stunning architecture and intricate designs. It is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is considered a symbol of love. Millions of tourists visit it every year to admire its beauty and historical importance.
